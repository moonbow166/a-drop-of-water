# A drop of water

*life on the space station · I* — how the International Space Station turns yesterday into tomorrow's coffee.

An animated, hand-drawn, step-by-step diagram of the ISS water loop: breath and sweat → cabin air → condensate; urine → vacuum distillation → brine; everything → the Water Processor Assembly → drinking water, with about 98% of the water recovered.

**Live:** https://moonbow166.github.io/a-drop-of-water/
